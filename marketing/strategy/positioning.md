# Positioning and messaging

## Positioning statement

For **Dhaka's players, organisers and turf owners** who now run their games through Facebook pages, WhatsApp groups and phone calls, **Sportzy** is the one place to **book a real open slot, run a paid tournament, and stream the match with a live scoreboard**. Unlike booking-only apps (Turfly, Turfs.bd) or scoring-only apps (CricHeroes), Sportzy connects the booking, the money and the broadcast, in Bangla and BDT, on bKash and Nagad.

## Brand idea: Floodlight Night

The floodlights come on across Dhaka every evening. That's when the city plays. Sportzy is how you get on the pitch tonight, and how everyone else gets to watch.

- Tagline (EN): **Your next match is one tap away.** (homepage headline, already in the product)
- Tagline (BN): **পরের ম্যাচ, এক ট্যাপ দূরে।**
- Tournament line (EN): **Run the tournament. We'll handle the money.**
- Tournament line (BN): **টুর্নামেন্ট আপনি চালান, টাকার হিসাব আমাদের।**
- Broadcast line (EN): **Every match deserves a broadcast.** (from the pitch deck)
- Broadcast line (BN): **প্রতিটা ম্যাচ লাইভ হওয়ার যোগ্য।**

## Voice

- Speak like a captain on a WhatsApp group: short, direct, warm, a little competitive.
- Bangla first for players, owners and organisers. Use the English sports words people actually say: turf, slot, booking, match, team, final, live.
- English for corporate, LinkedIn and diaspora.
- Numbers over adjectives: "৳1,500/hour, 9 PM free tonight" beats "affordable and convenient".
- Never invent statistics, testimonials or venue names in public content. Use `[Venue name]` until a signed venue agrees in writing.

## Bangla style notes for the copywriter

- Prefer the spoken form: "বুক করো" or "বুক করুন". Use **করুন** for owners and corporate, **করো** for players aged 16–30.
- Keep sport terms in English letters or Bangla transliteration, whichever looks natural: "টার্ফ", "স্লট", "ম্যাচ", "লাইভ".
- Avoid literal translations of English idioms. "Game on" isn't "খেলা চালু"; use "চল খেলি" or "মাঠে নামো".
