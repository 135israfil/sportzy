# Competitor analysis (marketing view)

The full research, with sources, is in the business review doc "Sportzy Business Model Review" (https://claude.ai/artifact/2M5X91aKEoyV4fHwB9G8H3). Prices and scale there come from public listings and third-party estimates seen in search results, dated 3 Nov 2026. Re-check before quoting any competitor in public, and **never name competitors in ads**.

| Competitor | What they sell to the customer | How we position against them (internal) |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook pages + WhatsApp + phone | Free; everyone already uses them | "Keep your WhatsApp group. Send them a Sportzy link instead of 20 messages." We sit inside WhatsApp, not against it. |
| Turfly (Dhaka) | Booking app with bKash, Nagad and cards; 23+ turfs per its store listing | Booking is table stakes. We win on tournaments, match pages and streams. |
| Turfs.bd (Dhaka, Chattogram) | Booking; "no monthly fee, commission only" | Same owner terms (commission only), plus tournaments that fill off-peak nights. |
| Khelbokoi, Turffin | Booking sites with promo codes and SEO guides | Don't fight on discounts. Content (guides, "Tonight at…") plus real availability. |
| TurfBook | Venue management software | Free owner tools plus demand; they give tools without players. |
| CricHeroes | Cricket scoring, stats, streaming (Pro about $24.99 a year; about $4.99 per streamed match) | Local, multi-sport (futsal and football), BDT on bKash and Nagad, linked to the venue booking and entry fees. |
| CameraFi, Switcher, Apollo, RECnGO | Phone streaming apps with scoreboards | We aren't selling camera software. We sell the match: booking, money, scoreboard and stream together. |

**Gaps to own in messaging:**

1. Tournament money in BDT, collected and refunded for the organiser.
2. Fixtures booked as real turf slots.
3. Bangla-first, futsal and football as well as cricket.
4. Sponsor-ready local broadcasts.
