These are illustrative composites for planning, not real people. Validate each one through the interviews in `video/scripts/08_testimonial_interview_guide.md` and the venue and organiser calls.

| Persona | Who | Where they are | Pain today | What makes them act | Channel | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner Ovi** (turf owner) | Runs 1–2 turfs in Mirpur, Bashundhara or Uttara. Manager handles phone bookings. | Facebook page for the turf; WhatsApp with regulars | 1–5 PM and weekday mornings empty; no-shows; cash through staff | Seeing a booking come in that he didn't have to chase; money paid weekly | In person, WhatsApp, Facebook DM | Free listing, 8% only on Sportzy bookings, payouts weekly; launch offer pending approval |
| **Captain Rafi** (player, 19–30) | Organises the Thursday-night futsal for office or university friends | Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok | Calling 3 turfs to find a free slot; chasing friends for money | Open slots shown with prices; a link to share with the squad | Meta ads, Instagram reels, WhatsApp shares | First booking campaign; "Bring your team" |
| **Joiner Nayeem** (solo player) | Moved to Dhaka for work; wants a game | Facebook groups ("Dhaka Football Lovers"-type), TikTok | Doesn't know a team | Find a game shows spots left tonight | Facebook groups, TikTok | Find your next game |
| **Organiser Sohel** (independent) | Runs 4–8 tournaments a year: futsal nights, Ramadan cups, cricket T10s | Facebook page and events, WhatsApp groups of captains | Collecting fees and screenshots from 12 captains; refunds and disputes; finding sponsors | Money collected and refunded for him; a stream for the final that sponsors can see | WhatsApp, phone, in person at events | 10% entry-fee collection + streams ৳300/game; Club & League ৳3,600/month |
| **HR Hasina** (corporate) | People and culture lead at a 200–2,000 person company | LinkedIn, email | Running an inter-department league takes weeks of admin | A turnkey season: fixtures, venue slots, a live final for the CEO | LinkedIn, email, warm intros | Corporate league package (price pending approval) |
| **Club Secretary Tanvir** (university) | Sports secretary at a private university club | Facebook, Instagram, campus WhatsApp | Inter-department tournament on a tiny budget | Free registration tools; streams for the final; a student rate | Campus visits, Instagram | University league offer |
| **Coach Mithu** (academy) | Runs a cricket or football academy with 40–150 kids | Facebook, WhatsApp with parents | Parents want to see matches; fees by hand | Streams of academy matches for parents | In person | Club & League plan (৳3,600/month) |
| **Viewer Shuvo** (audience) | Friends, family and fans of local players | Facebook video, YouTube | Hears about a final after it's over | A live link shared in the group | Facebook live, shares | Follow the page; book the same turf |
