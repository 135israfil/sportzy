# Brand guidelines: Floodlight Night

Source: the founder's "Sportzy Homepage Redesign" canvas, applied sitewide per SPOR-48 and SPOR-63. Logo files come from the "Sportzy Pitch Deck" artifact. **Founder to confirm**: are these the final logo files, and is there an SVG master?

## Logo

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `brand/sportzy-mark-web.svg` | The mark used on the web app: green circle with a dark pentagon. Use it for app-style UI, favicons and small sizes |
| `brand/sportzy-mark-pitchdeck.png` (1024²) | The football mark from the pitch deck: green circle with a black ball. Use it for social avatars and stickers |
| `brand/sportzy-wordmark-pitchdeck.png` (900×260, white) | Wordmark for dark backgrounds only |

- The web header sets "SPORTZY" in Anton, uppercase, 1.5 px letter-spacing, next to the mark. Recreate it like that in Canva if no vector wordmark is supplied.
- Clear space is the height of the mark on every side. Minimum size is 24 px tall for the mark and 96 px wide for the lockup.
- Don't recolour, stretch or outline the logo, or place it on busy photos without a dark scrim.
- **Open question for the founder:** the two marks differ (pentagon vs football). Pick one as primary. Until then, use the web pentagon for product contexts and the football for social avatars.

## Type

- **Anton:** headlines, uppercase, tight leading (0.92). Two lines max per post, with the second line in green.
- **Manrope 500–800:** body and buttons.
- **JetBrains Mono 500–700:** times, prices, scores (for example "9:00 PM", "৳1,500").
- **Bangla:** Anton has no Bangla glyphs. Use **Hind Siliguri 600–700** for Bangla headlines and body (the prototypes already use it). Keep Bangla headlines under 8 words.

## Visual language

- **Night, floodlights, real turf.** Diagonal beams of warm light (#F4E04D at 15–35% opacity) over the pitch.
- Pitch-line motifs drawn as 2 px white lines at 65% opacity, as in the product's art cards.
- Product screens always sit inside a phone or laptop frame, never as a full-bleed screenshot. See `product_screens/INDEX.md` for which screens are approved.
- People: real Dhaka players with consent. Generated images are only for mood shots with no identifiable venue and no fake brand signage.
- Avoid stock "fitness gym" imagery, emoji as decoration, purple gradients, and fake UI.

## Layout grid (social)

- 1080×1080 and 1080×1350: 72 px margins; headline in the top 40%; CTA pill bottom-left; logo bottom-right at 56 px.
- 1080×1920 stories: keep text inside 1080×1420, avoiding the top 250 px and bottom 250 px UI zones.
- YouTube thumbnail 1280×720: three words max, a face or the ball, yellow accent.
- Facebook cover 1640×624 (displays at 820×312 on desktop and crops on mobile): keep the logo and headline in the centre 1000×400.
