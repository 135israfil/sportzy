## 5. Keep / kill / scale rules

Apply these mechanically. Override one only with a written reason.

- **Kill:**
  - A paid ad set over the stop rule after ৳3,000 spent.
  - An organic format with reach in the bottom 25% for 3 posts in a row.
  - An outreach script with less than a 10% reply rate after 30 sends.
- **Keep:** a result within the rule but not yet 7 days of data. Change only one variable.
- **Scale:**
  - Cost under 70% of the stop rule for 7 days: raise budget by up to +20% a week.
  - An organic post in the top 10% for reach: boost it (needs the founder's OK on spend) or remake it in two new formats.
- **Experiment closed:** write the result into `strategy/budget_plan.md` § 5, then update the stop rule if the experiment set a real number.
