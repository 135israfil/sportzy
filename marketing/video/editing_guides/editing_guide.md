# Editing guide

| Platform | Aspect | Length | Safe zones | Export |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels / Facebook Reels | 9:16, 1080×1920 | 15–45 s | Keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px | H.264, 30 fps, about 12 Mbps, AAC 320 |
| TikTok | 9:16 | 15–30 s | Bottom 400 px is covered by UI | Same |
| YouTube | 16:9, 1920×1080 | 30–90 s | Thumbnail per DES-06 | H.264, about 16 Mbps |
| Feed video | 1:1 or 4:5 | 15–30 s | 72 px margins | Same |

**Rules:**

- The hook goes in frame 1. No slow logo intros. The end card lasts 2 s.
- Always burn in Bangla captions, because most people watch muted.
- Music: royalty-free tracks with a commercial licence only (keep the licence file next to the project). No copyrighted songs, including on Facebook.
- **Templates:** build CapCut or Canva templates for VID-09, VID-12 and VID-13 so each weekly cut takes 20 minutes.
- **Consent:** keep a signed release for every identifiable person and every venue. Store them in the shared drive under `consents/`.
- **Product footage:** live-site screen recordings only after go-live. Design renders appear only in pre-launch content, framed in a device and not presented as the finished app.
