# Content calendar: summary
The full calendar is `content_calendar.csv`: 400 rows from 2026-11-16 to 2027-05-14 (180 days). It has 104 feed posts, a story every day, and weekly sales, production and retention blocks. Open it in Google Sheets and filter by `Date` or `Campaign`.

## Rules
- **Launch-date slip:** if go-live (SPOR-14/15/16) isn't shipped by 11 Dec, every row marked `[LAUNCH]` moves by the same number of days. Before launch, fill the gap with STY-PRE stories and pre-launch problem posts.
- **Gates:** a row with a `Gate` value publishes only when that condition is true: the feature is live, consent is in hand, the number is real, the price is approved, or the paid tournament exists. Otherwise skip it, or swap in an evergreen post (CAP-002, CAP-005, CAP-020, CAP-036).
- **Brackets:** caption text in [brackets] is filled from real data on the day. Nothing is published with brackets left in.
- **Frequency:** 4 feed posts a week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat; the exceptions are national days), daily stories, and community replies within 2 hours from 10 AM to 11 PM.
