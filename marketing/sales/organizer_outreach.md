# Tournament organiser kit

**Goal:** 10 paid tournaments a month by May 2027.

**Paid tournament** = the organiser paid Sportzy for streams, a Club & League plan, or entry fees collected through Sportzy *(definition pending approval)*.

## 1. Introduction (WhatsApp or Facebook DM)

**বাংলা**

> আসসালামু আলাইকুম [নাম] ভাই। আপনার [Tournament]-এর পোস্ট দেখলাম। আমি [নাম], Sportzy থেকে। আমরা অর্গানাইজারদের জন্য একটা টুল বানিয়েছি: টিমগুলো অনলাইনে রেজিস্টার করে QR Pay BD-তে এন্ট্রি ফি দেয়, পে করার সময় স্পট হোল্ড থাকে, রিফান্ড আমরা সামলাই, ফিক্সচার আর মাঠের স্লট এক জায়গায়, আর ফাইনাল ফেসবুক-ইউটিউবে লাইভ। পরের টুর্নামেন্টটা একসাথে সেটআপ করি?

**English**

> Hi [name], I saw your [Tournament] post. I'm [name] from Sportzy. We built a tool for organisers: teams register online and pay entry fees by QR Pay BD, spots are held while they pay, we handle refunds, fixtures and turf slots sit in one place, and the final streams to Facebook and YouTube. Shall we set up your next tournament together?

## 2. The pitch (10-minute demo on their phone)

1. "How do you collect entry fees today? How many screenshots for your last event?"
2. Create a draft tournament with them: name, sport, fee, max teams, deadline.
3. Show the captain's side: register, pay, the 10-minute hold, and the refund if rejected.
4. Generate fixtures; book a slot for one fixture at a partner venue.
5. Show a Streemzy stream from a past match, and a Scorezy scorecard (beta).
6. Close: "চলুন আজই রেজিস্ট্রেশন খুলে দিই।" (Let's open registration today.)

## 5. Tournament promotional package (for every paid tournament)

- A DES-09 poster in three sizes, with the organiser's logo and a sponsor slot.
- A VID-09 promo reel (15 s) before registration closes.
- DES-10 fixtures and standings cards each round.
- Live stream of the final (paid streams), plus a VID-13 highlights recap.
- A Sportzy page post and story mentions on match days.

## 6. Sponsorship package (to help organisers sell)

| Slot | What the sponsor gets | Proof we provide |
|---|---|---|
| Title sponsor | Name on the tournament, poster logo, stream title, verbal mention | Stream views and reach per platform (from Streemzy), photo pack |
| Stream partner | Logo on the stream end card and in the post caption | View counts |
| Match sponsor | "Match of the day presented by…" | Post reach |

Revenue share on sponsorships Sportzy brings: *pending approval*. A suggested starting point is 20% to Sportzy.

## 7. Organiser onboarding guide

- [ ] Organiser access requested and approved (SPOR-100); organisation created in Sportzy ID
- [ ] Tournament created: name, sport, format, fee, max teams, deadline, cover, rules, prize
- [ ] Published; registration link shared in their captain groups
- [ ] Fixtures generated once teams are confirmed; slots booked at partner venues
- [ ] Crew assigned (scorer, streamer); stream test 30 minutes before kick-off
- [ ] Results entered; standings shared after each round
- [ ] After the event: VID-08 organiser interview (with consent), and an ask for the next date
