# Partnership proposals

## 1. One-page venue partnership proposal (DES-11 content)

**Sportzy × [Venue]: partnership proposal**

**What [Venue] gets:**

1. Free listing on Sportzy, so Dhaka players see your open slots with prices and book and pay online.
2. Every Sportzy booking paid online and collected by Sportzy, then paid to you weekly to a verified bank, bKash or Nagad account.
3. Tournament organisers who book runs of evenings at your normal prices.
4. An owner dashboard: today's bookings, walk-ins, blocked slots, discounts for slow hours, earnings.
5. Free marketing: a turf spotlight post, match-day stories and an in-venue poster.

**What it costs:** 8% of bookings that come through Sportzy. Walk-ins you record yourself carry no commission. *Launch offer (pending approval):* 0% on your first 20 Sportzy bookings.

**What we ask:** keep availability up to date, honour Sportzy bookings, and allow photos for marketing (optional).

**Next step:** a 10-minute setup on your phone.

**Contact:** [name], [phone], sportzy.live

## 2. Corporate league offer (pending price approval)

**For:** companies with 200–2,000 staff, offered through HR or the sports club.

**Format:**

- 6–12 teams over 4–8 weeks, weeknight evenings at partner turfs.
- Futsal, five-a-side football, or box cricket.

**Included:**

- registration and team lists
- fixtures and booked turf slots
- referee coordination (manual)
- a live-streamed final (Streemzy) with company branding
- standings, MVP and winners' certificates
- a photo and highlight pack for internal comms

**Pricing:** a per-league package plus turf slot costs at the venue's price. **Founder to set the price.** The planning range for discussion is ৳25,000–60,000 per league plus slots (assumption). Never publish a price before approval.

**Pitch email:**

> **Subject:** An inter-department league your team will turn up for
>
> Hi [Name], we run company leagues on Dhaka turfs end to end. We handle fixtures, venue slots, registration, and a final streamed live for the whole office. You choose the dates. Could we share a one-page plan for a [Feb/Mar/Apr] league?
>
> [Name], Sportzy

## 3. University and academy offer

- **University inter-department or inter-university:** free organiser tools, a student rate on streamed finals *(pending approval)*, and posters and reels made for the club.
- **Academies:** Club & League at ৳3,600 a month for up to 12 streamed games *(pending approval)*, so parents can watch every match.

## 4. Sponsor deck outline (for organisers' sponsors, 6 slides)

1. The audience: who plays and who watches. Use real follower and view numbers only.
2. The tournament: format, teams, dates, venue.
3. Placements: title, stream partner, match sponsor.
4. Proof: past stream views and reach, photos.
5. Price and deliverables.
6. Contact.

Build it in the Slides artifact type when the first sponsor conversation is scheduled.
