## Plan

- Clusters: A = Mirpur–Pallabi–Mohammadpur; B = Bashundhara–Badda–Gulshan; C = Uttara (Jan); D = Dhanmondi–Wari (Feb); E = Chattogram (Apr).
- Funnel: list → contact → visit/demo → signed → onboarded (availability verified) → active (first completed booking).
- Activation is the hard step: send each new venue its first booking through a tournament or a "Founding captain".
- Venue success stories: after 20 bookings, film VID-08 at that venue (with consent).
