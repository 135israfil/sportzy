# Turf owner outreach (pre-launch)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | 16 Nov – 11 Dec 2026 |
| **Objective** | Turn the 10 signed turfs into onboarded venues with verified availability; sign 5 more. |
| **Audience** | Turf owners and managers in Mirpur, Bashundhara, Uttara, Mohammadpur, Dhanmondi |
| **Offer and message** | Free listing, 8% only on Sportzy bookings, weekly payouts. Launch incentive pending approval: 0% on the first 20 Sportzy bookings. |
| **Feature status** | Built: owner signup, dashboard, payouts (SPOR-74, 85, 89). |
| **Channels** | In person, WhatsApp, phone, Facebook DM |
| **Assets** | sales/venue_outreach.md, DES-07 poster, DES-11 one-pager, VID-04, VID-14 |
| **Owner** | B2B Sales (C) |
| **KPI** | Venues onboarded with verified availability (target 15 by 11 Dec). |
| **Success** | 15 onboarded |
| **Kill or change if** | Fewer than 10 by 4 Dec: the founder joins every visit; extend to 3 field days a week. |

## Plan

- Day 1–5: visit all 10 signed venues; set availability with them on their phone (onboarding checklist).
- Day 6–20: visit the remaining ~10 documented Dhaka turfs, two clusters per day.
- Leave the DES-07 poster and table standee with the venue QR at every onboarded venue.
