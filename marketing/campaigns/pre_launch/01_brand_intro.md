# Brand introduction: "Floodlight Night"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | 16 Nov – 11 Dec 2026 |
| **Objective** | Make Sportzy recognisable before launch: what it is, who it is for, and that it is Dhaka-made. |
| **Audience** | Players 17–34, organisers and owners in Dhaka |
| **Offer and message** | None (awareness). |
| **Feature status** | Built: brand, product designs. No product claims beyond Built features. |
| **Channels** | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube (VID-01), LinkedIn (founder) |
| **Assets** | VID-01, DES-01, DES-05, DES-06, IMG-01, IMG-02, SCR main-desktop/mobile |
| **Owner** | Social (D) with Creative Director (E) |
| **KPI** | Follows are not traction. Measure 3-second video views from Dhaka, profile visits, and "notify me" WhatsApp opt-ins (target 300 by 11 Dec). |
| **Success** | 300 WhatsApp opt-ins; VID-01 50%+ of views watched to 3 s |
| **Kill or change if** | Under 100 opt-ins by 1 Dec: switch to organiser and venue content, which converts better pre-launch. |

## Plan

- Week 1: post VID-01 (brand film) and DES-01 carousel "One app for the whole match".
- Week 2: venue photo series (IMG-01/02) "Floodlights on across Dhaka".
- Pin the "Get notified at launch" WhatsApp link (wa.me link to the Sportzy business number) to every post.
