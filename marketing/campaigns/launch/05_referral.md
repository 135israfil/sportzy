# Referral programme

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | Manual from 1 Jan 2027; product version gated |
| **Objective** | Players and captains bring new bookers. |
| **Audience** | Players with 1+ completed booking |
| **Offer and message** | Manual version: refer a captain who completes a booking, and get your name drawn monthly for a free hour at a partner venue (venue-funded, pending venue agreement). Product version (codes, credits): **Gated: referral**. |
| **Feature status** | Not built (gated) |
| **Channels** | WhatsApp, Instagram stories |
| **Assets** | DES-18 |
| **Owner** | Growth (B) |
| **KPI** | Referred first bookings |
| **Success** | 20 referred bookings a month by March |
| **Kill or change if** | Under 5 a month by February: drop the draw, keep only organiser referrals. |

## Plan

- Track referrals by asking "Who told you about Sportzy?" in the booking confirmation WhatsApp (manual).
