FAQ

Streemzy questions

Is Streemzy free?

Yes. Streemzy is free for everyone during the Sportzy beta.

Can I stream to Facebook and YouTube at the same time?

Yes. Streemzy simulcasts one live stream to Facebook, YouTube and Instagram simultaneously.

What cameras work with Streemzy?

Any smartphone or camera that can send an RTMP stream works with Streemzy, so you can start with the phone in your pocket and upgrade later.

Can commentators join remotely?

Yes. Streemzy supports remote commentator dial-in, so commentary can come from anywhere.
