The problem

Almost no grassroots match is ever broadcast.

Roughly four million organized amateur matches are played every year. Almost none are streamed — not because nobody would watch, but because professional broadcast gear and crews cost tens of thousands of dollars.

Expensive equipment

Cameras, switchers and production gear are priced for TV networks, not clubs.

No technical crew

Local organizers don't have trained operators, directors or commentators.

No graphics

Scoreboards, lineups and sponsor overlays usually need dedicated software.

No replay or review

Instant replay and officiating tech exist only at the professional tier.

How it works

From kick-off to live stream in five steps.

Sportzy replaces a broadcast truck, a production crew and a rack of gear with software that runs on smartphones and the cloud.

  1. Create a match

    Set up teams and the fixture in under a minute.

  2. Connect a camera

    Use your phone, an action cam or any RTMP camera.

  3. Add live graphics

    Turn on scoreboards, lineups and lower thirds with Scorezy.

  4. Go live everywhere

    Stream to Facebook, YouTube and Instagram at once with Streemzy.

  5. Let AI help Soon

    Visionzy will auto-detect key moments and build highlights.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Sportzy really free?

Yes. Scorezy and Streemzy are free for everyone during the beta. Sign up and start broadcasting your matches today. Any future pricing will be announced on this website.

What do I need to live stream a match?

A smartphone (or any camera that supports RTMP) and a stable internet connection. Scorezy adds the scoreboard and graphics, and Streemzy sends the stream to your Facebook, YouTube and Instagram pages.

Can I stream to Facebook, YouTube and Instagram at the same time?

Yes. Streemzy multistreams one live feed to Facebook, YouTube and Instagram simultaneously, so your fans can watch wherever they already are.

Can I add a live cricket scoreboard to my stream?

Yes. Scorezy renders a live cricket scorecard, scoreboards, team lineups, lower thirds, match stats and sponsor graphics on your stream in real time.

Which sports does Sportzy support?

Scorezy has dedicated cricket scorecards and general scoreboards for football and other sports. Streemzy works with any sport — if you can film it, you can stream it.

When will Visionzy and the Sportzy app be available?

Both are in development. Visionzy will add AI ball tracking, LBW and VAR-style review and auto highlights; the Sportzy app will turn multiple phones into synchronized camera angles. Join the waitlist on their pages to hear first.

Who builds Sportzy?

Sportzy is a sports-tech startup founded in Bangladesh by Israfil Islam, on a mission to make professional sports broadcasting accessible to every league, club and academy.
