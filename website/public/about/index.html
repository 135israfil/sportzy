About Sportzy

Making professional sports technology accessible to everyone.

Sportzy is the operating system for grassroots sports broadcasting — turning any smartphone into a professional broadcast studio with live scoring, streaming and AI-powered sports intelligence, unified in one platform.

Our mission

We exist to close the gap between professional-grade sports technology and the organizations that have always been priced out of it — national federations, cricket boards, clubs, academies, schools and grassroots leagues.

We're starting in Bangladesh and building toward the emerging sports markets of South Asia, the Middle East, Africa and Southeast Asia, where the need is greatest and legacy broadcast systems are most out of reach.

Why we built it

Most sports organizations juggle disconnected tools for scoring, streaming and analytics, while technology like Hawk-Eye and broadcast production systems stays locked behind enterprise price tags. The result: limited fan engagement, missed sponsorship, and grassroots talent that goes undiscovered because nobody captured the match.

Meanwhile, the phones already in everyone's pockets are more capable than a lot of that gear. Sportzy is built on that gap.
