Guide

How to live stream a grassroots football match

  1. Find a high, central spot — halfway line, as high as possible, so the whole pitch fits in frame.
  2. Create the match in Scorezy — add teams, lineups and your competition branding.
  3. Connect Streemzy — link your Facebook page, YouTube channel and Instagram.
  4. Go live — update goals and the score as they happen; graphics appear instantly on stream.
  5. Bring in commentary — a commentator can dial in remotely to call the game.

Why stream your matches?

Parents and supporters who can't travel can watch live, scouts can see players in real games, sponsors get visibility on every broadcast, and every match is recorded in the cloud for highlights and review.

FAQ

Football streaming questions

Can I live stream a football match from my phone for free?

Yes. During the Sportzy beta, Streemzy and Scorezy are free, so you can stream from a phone with live score graphics at no cost.

Can I show the score and team lineups on my stream?

Yes. Scorezy adds a live scoreboard, team lineups, lower thirds and sponsor graphics to your football stream.

Can I stream to multiple platforms?

Yes. Streemzy streams your match to Facebook, YouTube and Instagram simultaneously.

Will there be VAR-style replays?

Visionzy, coming soon, will add VAR-style review, offside analysis, slow-motion replay and automatic highlights.
