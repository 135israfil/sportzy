FAQ

Scorezy questions

Is Scorezy free?

Yes. Scorezy is free for everyone during the Sportzy beta.

Does Scorezy support cricket scoring?

Yes. Scorezy includes cricket-specific scorecards with runs, wickets, overs, run rate and targets, alongside general scoreboards for other sports.

Can I add my own tournament logo and sponsors?

Yes. Scorezy has tournament branding controls and sponsor graphics so every broadcast carries your league's identity and your sponsors' logos.

Do I need Streemzy to use Scorezy?

No, but they're designed to work together: Scorezy creates the graphics, and Streemzy sends the finished broadcast to Facebook, YouTube and Instagram at the same time.
