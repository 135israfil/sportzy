Guide

How to live stream a local cricket match

You don't need a broadcast van to stream cricket. With a phone on a tripod behind the bowler's arm, a stable mobile or Wi-Fi connection and Sportzy, one person can run a complete broadcast:

  1. Set up the match in Scorezy — add both teams, playing XIs and the format (T10, T20, ODI or custom overs).
  2. Mount your camera — the classic straight-on view from behind the bowler's arm gives viewers the best angle.
  3. Connect Streemzy — link your club's Facebook page, YouTube channel and Instagram.
  4. Go live and score ball-by-ball — runs, wickets, overs, run rate and target update on screen instantly.
  5. Share the recording — the full match is saved in the cloud for replays and highlights.

What your viewers see

A broadcast-style cricket scorecard with batting team score, wickets, overs, current run rate and required rate in chases — plus team lineups, player name captions, tournament branding and sponsor logos. It's the look fans know from TV, applied to your match.

Why stream your cricket?

Families and fans abroad can watch live, sponsors get on-screen visibility, players get footage of their performances, and your tournament builds a following on the platforms people already use every day.
