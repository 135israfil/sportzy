FAQ

Tournament streaming questions

How much does it cost to live stream a tournament with Sportzy?

Scorezy and Streemzy are free for everyone during the Sportzy beta, so you can stream your whole tournament at no cost.

Can I put sponsor logos on my tournament live stream?

Yes. Scorezy includes sponsor graphics and tournament branding controls for every broadcast.

Which sports can I stream?

Scorezy has dedicated cricket scorecards plus scoreboards for football and other sports, and Streemzy can stream any sport you can film.

Can we stream several matches on the same day?

Yes. Each ground can stream from its own phone or camera, and each match can go to your tournament's Facebook, YouTube and Instagram.
