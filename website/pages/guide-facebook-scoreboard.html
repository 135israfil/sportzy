How to add a live scoreboard to Facebook Live

Facebook is where many local clubs and tournaments already have their audience. Here's how to add a scoreboard to Facebook Live: show a real-time score, team lineups and sponsor logos on your Facebook Live sports stream — without a PC, OBS or a graphics operator.

Updated 12 October 2026 · 5 min read

Why a scoreboard matters on Facebook Live

Most people join a live match halfway through, often on a phone with the sound off. Without a scoreboard they don't know who's winning and scroll away. A clear on-screen score keeps them watching — and makes your stream look like real TV, which makes it easier to attract sponsors.

The usual way (and why it's hard)

Traditionally you'd need a laptop running streaming software such as OBS, a capture setup for the camera, and someone updating score graphics by hand. That's a lot of equipment and skill for a local match.

The simple way: Scorezy + Streemzy

  1. Create the match in Scorezy — add teams, players and your tournament branding.
  2. Choose your overlays — scoreboard, cricket scorecard, lineups, lower thirds and sponsor graphics.
  3. Connect your camera — your phone or any RTMP camera.
  4. Add your Facebook Page in Streemzy as a destination (add YouTube and Instagram too if you like).
  5. Go live and update the score — every tap in Scorezy updates the scoreboard on Facebook in real time.

What you can show on screen

  • Live scoreboard for football and other sports
  • Full cricket scorecard: runs, wickets, overs, run rate, target
  • Team lineups and squads
  • Lower thirds for players, commentators and officials
  • Tournament logo, colors and sponsor graphics

Tips for Facebook Live sports streams

  • Stream from your club or tournament Page so fans can follow it and get notified next time.
  • Announce the stream with a post a day before, with the match time.
  • Ask players to share the live video — shares are the fastest way to reach new viewers.
  • Pin a comment with the fixture list or the next match time.
