How to live stream a cricket match on YouTube (with a live scoreboard)

Want to live stream cricket on YouTube? You can broadcast a local match with nothing more than a smartphone, a tripod and a data connection. This guide walks through the setup, the best camera angle and how to add a TV-style scorecard — free.

Updated 12 October 2026 · 6 min read

What you need

  • A smartphone (or any camera that can send an RTMP stream).
  • A tripod or stable mount — ideally raised above head height.
  • A stable internet connection — 4G/5G or Wi-Fi with steady upload speed. Test it at the ground before match day.
  • A power bank — a full T20 can drain a phone battery.
  • A YouTube channel with live streaming enabled.
  • Scorezy for the live scorecard and Streemzy to send the stream to YouTube — both free during beta.

Step 1: Enable live streaming on your YouTube channel

In YouTube Studio, open Create → Go live. The first time you do this, YouTube may ask you to verify your channel with a phone number and can take up to 24 hours to activate live streaming — so do it a day or more before your match.

Step 2: Set up the match in Scorezy

Create the match in Scorezy: add both teams, playing XIs, the toss and the format (T10, T20, 50 overs or custom). Add your tournament logo and any sponsor graphics so they appear on the stream.

Step 3: Position the camera

The classic cricket broadcast angle is straight down the pitch from behind the bowler's arm, raised as high as you safely can. It shows the line of the ball, the batter's shot and the field placement. Lock the phone in landscape and keep it out of the sightscreen area so it doesn't distract the batter.

Step 4: Connect YouTube in Streemzy

In Streemzy, add YouTube as a destination. If you also want to reach fans on Facebook and Instagram, add them too — Streemzy will stream to all of them at the same time.

Step 5: Go live and score ball-by-ball

Start the stream and score each delivery in Scorezy. Runs, wickets, overs, run rate and the required rate in a chase update on screen instantly, so viewers always know the state of the game.

Step 6: After the match

YouTube keeps the stream as a video on your channel, and Streemzy also records it in the cloud. Share the link in team groups, tag players, and cut highlights of the big moments.

Tips for a better cricket stream

  • Do a 5-minute private test stream at the ground before the first ball.
  • Give the stream a searchable title, e.g. "Team A vs Team B — Tournament Name 2026 — Live".
  • Shield the phone from direct sun to avoid overheating.
  • Bring in a commentator with remote dial-in — commentary keeps viewers watching longer.

FAQ

Cricket on YouTube — questions

Can I live stream cricket on YouTube from my phone?

Yes. Point your phone at the pitch, add a live scorecard with Scorezy and send the stream to YouTube with Streemzy. Both tools are free during the Sportzy beta.

How do I show the cricket score on my YouTube live stream?

Use Scorezy. It overlays a live cricket scorecard — runs, wickets, overs, run rate and target — on your stream and updates it as you score each ball.

Can I stream the same cricket match to YouTube and Facebook together?

Yes. Streemzy multistreams one live feed to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram at the same time.

What internet speed do I need to live stream cricket?

You need a steady upload connection; higher and more stable upload speeds allow better video quality. Always test the connection at the ground before the match.
