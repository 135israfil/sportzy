Sports App

Every match deserves a broadcast.

Sports App is the operating system for grassroots sports broadcasting — turning any smartphone into a professional broadcast studio with live scoring, streaming, and AI-powered sports intelligence, unified in one platform.

What We Aim To Do

Make professional sports technology accessible to every league, club, and academy.

Sports App's purpose is to close the gap between professional-grade sports technology and the organizations that have always been priced out of it — national federations, cricket boards, clubs, academies, schools, and grassroots leagues.

We're starting in Bangladesh and building toward the emerging sports markets of South Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia, where the need is greatest and the cost of legacy broadcast systems is most prohibitive.

What It Solves

99% of sports matches are never professionally broadcast.

Most sports organizations rely on disconnected tools for scoring, broadcasting, and analytics — while professional-grade technology like Hawk-Eye and broadcast production systems stays locked behind enterprise price tags. The result: inefficient match operations, limited fan engagement, and grassroots talent that goes undiscovered because no one captured the data.

Expensive equipment
Cameras, switchers, and production gear cost tens of thousands of dollars.
No technical crew
Local organizers lack trained operators, directors, and commentators.
No graphics systems
Scoreboards, lineups, and sponsor overlays require dedicated software.
No replay or review
Instant replay and officiating tools exist only at the professional tier.

Every weekend, thousands of matches happen with zero broadcast coverage — and zero data captured.

How We Solve It

One connected platform. Phones organizers already own.

Sports App replaces a broadcast truck, a production crew, and a rack of expensive gear with software that runs on smartphones and the cloud. Organizers create a match, connect a few phones as camera feeds, and go live — with real-time graphics, multi-platform streaming, and AI-generated highlights handled automatically.

The Platform & Its Three Engines

One app. Three connected engines.

Sports App is the central platform — an Android & iOS app that unifies the match-day experience. Underneath it, three purpose-built engines handle graphics, streaming, and AI intelligence — sold together or standalone, and designed to work as one system.

Scoring App Broadcast Graphics Engine Market Ready

Scoring App is Sports App's live scoring and broadcast-graphics engine — the layer that turns a raw camera feed into a professional-looking broadcast. It manages match data and renders live scoring graphics, animations, and tournament branding overlays on screen in real time.

Capabilities: live scoring graphics & animations, tournament branding overlay controls, scoreboards, team lineups & rosters, lower thirds, match statistics overlays, cricket scorecards, sponsor & brand graphics.
Stream Cloud Streaming Infrastructure Market Ready

Stream Cloud is the delivery layer — professional live streaming, broadcasting, and content distribution to multiple platforms and multiple locations at once. It takes video from any camera or phone and pushes it live everywhere an organizer needs, without a technical crew on site.

Capabilities: multi-platform simulcast to Facebook, YouTube & Instagram at once, multi-location streaming from a single match, RTMP ingest from any camera, multi-camera contribution, remote commentator dial-in, cloud recording & VOD.
Ai vision AI Sports Intelligence & Review Engine R&D Initiated

Ai vision uses AI-powered computer vision to turn every broadcast into structured sports data — ball and player tracking, officiating support, and performance analytics that were previously available only at the professional tier.

Capabilities: AI-powered ball tracking for LBW review, slow-mo instant replay, VAR-style & goal-line technology for review and playback on the live broadcast, auto highlight generation, event detection, player tracking, offside analysis, tactical & performance analytics.
Sports App The Central Platform Android & iOS App

Sports App is the central platform that connects and manages Scoring App, Stream Cloud, and Ai vision from a single Android or iOS app. Its most distinctive capability: it connects multiple phones over a local network to simulate multi-angle camera availability — turning every phone on the sideline into a live camera angle, with no dedicated broadcast hardware.

Capabilities: match setup & management, multi-phone local-network camera sync, unified control of all three engines, built for a single organizer or small volunteer team to run.
